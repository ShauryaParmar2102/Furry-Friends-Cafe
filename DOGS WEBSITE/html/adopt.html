How the Adoption Process Works

At Furry Friends Café, we make the adoption process simple and heartwarming. Our goal is to find the right home for every pet.

  1. Browse the available dogs and choose a pet you're interested in adopting.
  2. Visit Furry Friends Café and request an adoption application form.
  3. Complete the form with details about your lifestyle and home environment.
  4. Our team will review your application and arrange a meet-and-greet.
  5. If you already have pets, we encourage a trial introduction to check compatibility.
  6. Once everything is approved, you can welcome your new furry friend home!

If you have any questions about the adoption process, our friendly team is here to help.
